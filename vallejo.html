28 Vallejo Street

28 Vallejo Street is a fabulous Mediterranean, built in the Santa Fé style. Lovely views of the San Francisco Bay complement wonderful entertaining spaces, a large family / media room, and an eat in kitchen with Berkeley Mills cabinetry. This home is Trejo Design's most recent work, built as a spec house.

The images on this page are copyright David Feix, who we will miss sorely as he passed away in February 2025. We've archived images of his masterful work so they won't be lost. Image captions were written by him.
